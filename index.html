                  Daily goals

                  Calories your body burns at rest (BMR). Used for the deficit when the day's source only reports active calories. Garmin reports both, so you can leave this empty if you use Garmin.

                  Garmin

                  Pull active and resting calories from Garmin Connect to see your daily deficit or surplus. You need to be signed in above.

                  1. On a computer, open Garmin Connect → Settings → Personal API and create a key (pick 1 year).
                  2. Copy the key and paste it here. Garmin only shows it once.

                  The key is saved to your account, so every device you sign in on can pull from Garmin. The app never shows it again; to change it, paste a new one.

                  Food database

                  Search uses USDA FoodData Central and Open Food Facts. USDA works with a shared demo key that is rate limited; get your own free key at fdc.nal.usda.gov and paste it here.
